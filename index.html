Hi, I'm Haley!

Clinical Research Coordinator @ Autism Assessment, Research, Treatment, and Services Center (AARTS), Rush University Medical Center

B.S. Psychological Sciences & B.A. Spanish, Purdue University

I am interested in studying behavioral profiles among children with developmental disabilities to understand their individual strengths and functional challenges. Through my current role as a Clinical Research Coordinator for a telehealth-based behavioral intervention RCT, I have seen how multiple measures can capture different aspects of the same behavior, which is especially important for informing intervention targets that are both clinically meaningful and family-centered.

I have coordinated clinical research, led first-author poster projects, and supported manuscript development, contributing to my interest in measurement research among children with autism, developmental disabilities, and genetic syndromes. I plan to pursue a PhD in Clinical Psychology to build on these research interests and experiences.

Experience

Current Role

Clinical Research Coordinator for a behavioral RCT (WINGS) examining the efficacy and implementation of a caregiver-coached, telehealth-based challenging behavior intervention tailored for children with genetic syndromes and co-occurring intellectual disability.

Research Experience

Clinical and community-focused research experience spanning autism and developmental disability populations, including children with genetic syndromes, with a focus on examining behavioral profiles and different measurement approaches.

Clinical Experience

Experience implementing evidence-based social skills and behavioral interventions, such as PEERS, the Summer Treatment Program, and NETT, with adolescents and children with autism and developmental disabilities.
